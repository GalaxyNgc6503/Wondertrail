Wondertrail

A travel discovery app: roll or choose a trail, contribute your own. This is a browser demo of the Flutter app, running on sample places in Sibu, Sarawak.

  • casinoRoll — tap pins to mark Must Visit stops, pick a trail length, then roll the rest of the route around them.
  • add_circleBuild — add a destination: drop a pin, fill in the details, preview it and publish.
  • workspace_premiumPremium — 3 free rolls a day; Upgrade unlocks unlimited rolls and single-stop re-rolls (no payment in this demo).

Sample data only. Everything resets when you reload.
